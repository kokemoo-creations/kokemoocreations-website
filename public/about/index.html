About Me

Nyaho! I’m Koki!

I’m a multimedia artist with a background in Mathematics and Computer Science, and a special interest in bringing technology into artistic spaces (no Generative AI though). I’m a camera and gear nerd juggling way too many hobbies for my own good, and am always up for learning new skills. You’ll often catch me experimenting with new techniques or pushing the boundaries of whatever medium I’m trying out at the time.

I enjoy working on projects where I can bring out my silliness and make people laugh, but I also find a lot of fulfillment in working on projects bringing awareness to systemic barriers and issues facing marginalized communities. As a transgender, disabled, POC, I try my best to bring representation and opportunities to others in marginalized groups through my work, and through collaborations.
